# Tigu Serategna

### Connecting Local Services, People, and Job Opportunities

**Tigu Serategna** is a local service network designed to connect service providers with people who need their services. It also helps companies, hotels, schools, and other organizations share job opportunities so users can discover and apply for jobs near their locations.

Our goal is to make finding services and local employment opportunities easier, more accessible, and more secure.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Local Service Network:** Connect service providers with customers looking for their services.
- **Job Opportunities:** Enable companies, hotels, schools, and other organizations to publish job vacancies.
- **Location-Based Job Discovery:** Help users find relevant job opportunities near their locations.
- **Job Applications:** Allow users to apply for available positions through the platform.
- **Phone Verification:** Support phone-number verification to help improve account security and trust.
- **Growing Network:** Build a platform that brings service providers, customers, and employers together in one place.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To connect people with reliable local services and accessible job opportunities through a convenient, location-aware digital platform.
